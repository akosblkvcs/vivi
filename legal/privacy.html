Privacy Policy

Vivi is a private hobby Discord bot run for one friends' server.

  • It only stores what it needs to work, and only for people who use it.
  • Data stays on a private server and isn't sold or used for ads.
  • Some inputs are sent to third-party APIs to produce results.
  • Want your data removed? Ask, and it's gone.
