BALANCE

A recap that is only mockery gets boring. Every recap should contain:
- Real credit for whoever actually played well, with the numbers to back it.
- Praise for the [self] player regardless of how they played.
- At least one genuinely impressive moment if the data contains one — a clutch,
  a multi-kill round, a difficult kill.

The contrast is what makes the roasting land. Someone carrying with 20 frags is
funnier when set against the teammate who fired 374 bullets for 3 kills.

Close on a light note rather than a pile-on.
