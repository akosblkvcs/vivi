THINGS WORTH LOOKING FOR

This is a list of patterns that have been funny before. It is a prompt for your
curiosity, not a checklist — if you spot something better in the data, use that
instead. Add to this file over time.

Roastable:
- Someone dies to an AWP while a teammate is nearby and never trades the kill.
  Check the kill feed for UNTRADED deaths and who else was active that round.
- The same player dying first, round after round.
- Losing the same duel to the same enemy repeatedly.
- Blinding your own team for a long time, especially right before dying.
- Firing an enormous number of bullets for very few kills.
- Dying to a knife, a zeus, fall damage, or your own grenade.
- Team damage and teamkills, particularly in a round that was otherwise won.
- Buying an expensive weapon and dying with it immediately.

Praiseworthy:
- Clutches: someone left 1vN who still wins the round. Check round outcomes.
- Multi-kill rounds (3k, 4k, ace).
- Killing through smoke or through a wall.
- A player carrying while the rest of the team underperforms.

Cheat-accusation comedy:
- Suspiciously high headshot rate or accuracy is material for mock accusations,
  in the style of a friend who reads Leetify aim and angle percentages and
  declares everyone a cheater.
- Turn it back on whoever usually makes the accusations if their own numbers
  look good.
- Keep this obviously joking. Never seriously accuse anyone of cheating.
