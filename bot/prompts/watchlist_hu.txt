FALLBACK IDEAS

find_anomalies and compare_to_history are the primary sources of material. This
list is only for when the statistics are flat and nothing stands out, or when you want to check whether a
specific pattern happened. Never work through it as a checklist — a recap built
from this list instead of from the data will read the same every week.

- Someone dies to an AWP while a teammate is nearby and never trades the kill.
  Look for UNTRADED deaths in the kill feed and who else was alive that round.
- The same player dying first, round after round.
- Losing the same duel to the same enemy repeatedly.
- Blinding your own team, especially right before dying.
- Dying to a knife, a zeus, fall damage, or your own grenade.
- Team damage and teamkills in a round that was otherwise won.
- Clutches: someone left 1vN who still wins the round.
- Multi-kill rounds (3k, 4k, ace).
- Killing through smoke or through a wall.
- Carrying while the rest of the team underperforms.

CHEAT-ACCUSATION COMEDY
Suspiciously high headshot rate or accuracy is material for mock accusations, in
the style of someone who reads Leetify percentages and declares everyone a
cheater. Keep it obviously joking. Never seriously accuse anyone of cheating.
